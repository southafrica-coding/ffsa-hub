Notes for the build
How these pieces behave. Every later Missio turn assumes them.

Frame

From ≥1024px it's the sidebar. Below that it's the top bar, bottom tabs and a drawer. The tab bar is fixed and respects env(safe-area-inset-bottom). Content gets matching bottom padding so the last card never hides behind the bar. Nothing scrolls sideways at 360, 390 or 430px. Long names wrap or truncate with an ellipsis inside the card.

Switching org

Desktop: tap the org tile to open a popover with two rows, then tap one. Phone: the two-way toggle at the top of the drawer. Either way you get the paper and wordmark beat, then the new frame's Home. The last org is stored per user, so the Hub opens where you left it. Never switch automatically from a deep link. If a link belongs to the other org, show "This is in FFSA. Switch?"

Sheets & drawer

Use a native <dialog> with a scrim. Focus is trapped inside, Esc closes it, and focus goes back to whatever opened it. Sheets snap to their content height, up to 90% of the screen, and the action sits in a sticky footer. On desktop the same content opens as a 480px side panel on the right, not a centred modal.

Press & hover

Every tappable thing has a 120ms press (scale 0.98 and a darker fill). Linked cards lift from s1 to s2 on hover, and only on devices with a real pointer (@media (hover:hover)). Focus rings appear on :focus-visible only.

Loading

Show skeletons shaped like the real rows, never a spinner over the whole page. Home cards load in independently. A failed card shows its own error with Try again and leaves the other three alone. Numbers keep their last known value while they refresh.

Fonts & tokens

Load Fraunces (opsz + wght axes) and Archivo through next/font, scoped to the Missio skin so FFSA doesn't download them. Tokens go in as CSS variables under [data-org="missio"], mapped in Tailwind as missio-* colours, radii and shadows.
