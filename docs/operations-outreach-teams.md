# Operations → Visiting Teams — plan

Draft, 24 Nov 2026. Built from Mache van Zyl's "FFSA: Hosting Outreach Teams" (Google Doc and Canva timeline, Sept 2026). Mache leads this, with Michelle Strydom. Nothing built yet.

**Visiting teams** are teams that come to FFSA to be hosted. (In the Hub, "outreach teams" already means *our own* teams going out — Finance → Outreach budgets — so the two are kept apart by name.)

## What it's for

Mache's process is already clear: ten steps, split between the **outreach leader** (ORL, the visiting team's leader) and the **host** (FFSA's contact person), over three periods: **pre-hosting, inter-hosting, post-hosting**.

What's hard is keeping track of *which team is at which step*, and telling Finance, Hospitality, the kitchen parents, Transport and Legal at the right moment. The Hub carries that:

- every team is a **record** with its dates, size, leader and needs — and Mache can change any of it, any time
- every team gets **Mache's checklist**, grouped by stage, with dates counted back from arrival
- the checklist **moves forward as information comes in**
- the **07:00 message** shows each team's stage and what's still needed to finish it
- departments get told at the right moment — **but only after Mache approves the message**

The calls, WhatsApp group, pastoral care and thank-yous stay personal. The Hub reminds; it doesn't talk for her.

## Decided (Gideon, 24 Nov 2026)

- Name: **Visiting teams**, under the Operations department.
- **Every automatic notice is approved by Mache before it goes.** The Hub drafts it; she reads, edits if needed, and sends (in Slack or on the Hub). Nothing goes out on its own.
- The checklist moves forward as information arrives, but **arrangements change often**: Mache can edit every detail and every answer a team has submitted. When something changes, the old value stays in the team's history ("Arrival moved from 12 Mar to 14 Mar — Mache, 3 Feb"), and dates that hang off it move with it.
- The 07:00 message shows **each team's stage and what's still open for that stage**.
- A team can end as **Said no**, **Postponed** or **Cancelled**, not only Left.
- Team information is kept for **12 months** after the team leaves or ends (to be confirmed with Coreen, Michelle and Mache).

## Stages and the checklist

The stages follow Mache's timeline. Each stage holds her steps for it; the stage is finished when its list is.

| Stage | Mache's steps | Still needed = |
|---|---|---|
| **Enquiry** | 1–2 first contact · 3 set-up (WhatsApp group, Welcoming Guide, schedule call) · 4 call with ORL (heart/vision, why SA, locations, expectations) | open items from 1–4 |
| **Confirmed** | 5 confirm: yes/no, dates, locations/ministries, contact ministries, share Team Info + Legal forms, visas → Legal, Finance → sheet, wire-transfer deadline, Hospitality heads-up | forms not back yet, departments not told |
| **Preparing** | 6 forms received: invoice, rooms, tell the kitchen parents + fridge/cupboard space, book vehicles, airport pickup (2½–3 weeks before), ministry schedule + contacts, briefings, welcome packets & prophetic words, first-day meals, packing list / cultural training, Q&As · 7 pre-arrival: base rules/orientation/map on WhatsApp, rooms (Potch), connect with kitchen parents + drivers, team updates | each open item, with its date |
| **Here** | 8 arrival: meet & greet, drivers test, settle in / buy food, orientation, ministry/legal briefings & forms, ministry schedule run-through · pastoral care (face to face first 1–3 days, weekly touch point) · 9 before departure: outstanding fees, vehicles filled & clean, keys & housing, fridge & cupboard space cleared, airport drop-off, testimonies/feedback forms, thank-yous | open items; this week's touch point |
| **Left** | 10 feedback from Finance, Hospitality, kitchen parents, ministry contacts → feedback to ORL · thank-yous / stay connected | departments not heard from |
| *Said no / Postponed / Cancelled* | — | nothing; drops out of the morning message |

"Moves forward as information comes in" means:

- Some items tick themselves when the information they ask for is on the team (e.g. flights entered → *arrival times received*). Mache can untick.
- When a stage's list is done, the Hub **suggests** moving to the next stage; Mache confirms. She can also move a team on with items still open — they stay visible.
- Date-based items (airport pickup 3 weeks before, pre-arrival the week before, departure prep 3 days before) recalculate when arrival or departure dates change.

## The 07:00 message

One message in the visiting-teams channel, only on days when something is due. Per active team:

> **Team Kansas City** · 14 people · arrives Thu 12 Mar · *Preparing*
> Still needed: airport pickup (due 19 Feb) · kitchen parents told · welcome packets

Plus arrivals and departures this week. Notices waiting for Mache's approval are listed for her in a DM.

## What Mache already has (Drive, read 24 Nov 2026)

Mache sent three things on Slack (DM, 24 Nov): the process document, a **Mastersheet**, and a Drive folder. Together they're most of a handover pack already.

- **Mastersheet** — one tab per resource, each with a note on what it is, when she uses it and why:
  - *Communication platforms*: the shared teams@ mailbox; WhatsApp groups with each team's leaders.
  - *Welcome packet*: welcome card and info QR card (Canva), base map, room-cleaning checklist.
  - *Finance*: one sheet of budgets invoiced to outreach leaders, a tab per team.
  - *Communication to leaders*: the **Welcoming Guide** (two versions: SA + Potch, Potch only) — the first thing she sends; the two Google Forms and the WhatsApp message she sends with them; packing-list examples; an English questionnaire for teams whose first language isn't English; Potch ministry opportunities; the two **waiver forms** every team member signs on day one (required to stay on base).
  - *Hosting dates 2026/2027*: the list of teams.
  - *Travel schedule* (teams going beyond Potch) and *Potch ministry schedule* templates, with past teams' schedules.
  - *SA contacts*: partner orientation documents (e.g. a Johannesburg church).
- **Hosting dates 2026/2027** — columns: status (*Confirmed · In progress · Not coming anymore*), outreach dates (often rough: "April 2027", "9/11 Jan – 5/6 Mar"), name (base, school, church), contact + role, team size (often a range), team details, locations in SA, base vehicle rental (priced per car/van per day), room allocations on the Potch base.
- **Form 1, Team Details & Essentials** (sent once a team is confirmed) — where from; up to 3 team leaders; arrival + departure; number of members; men and women with names and emails; married couples and children; skills; **dietary needs and allergies**; **medical conditions**; base vehicle application (which option, which dates); other places in SA; pastoral visit (who, when); debrief (when, where); school leader / field coordinator; comments.
- **Form 2, Team Travelling Details** — flights and arrival, filled in once flights are booked.

What this means for the build:

- The team record follows her sheet's columns, so the 2026/2027 list can be brought in as it is. **Dates and team size can be rough at first** ("April 2027", "10–12") and get firm later.
- Her statuses map to ours: *In progress* → Enquiry, *Confirmed* → Confirmed, *Not coming anymore* → Cancelled / Said no / Postponed.
- The two forms become Hub forms (bit 4); until then, Mache can paste or type answers in, and everything stays editable.
- **Medical and dietary information is sensitive.** Both are seen only by the host and the area lead. There is no base kitchen (kitchen parents look after the shared kitchen; everyone makes their own meals), so dietary needs matter for the first-day meals and for partner locations that cater — the host passes on only what each needs. Both are cleared when the keeping period ends.
- The Mastersheet's resources (Welcoming Guide, forms, packing lists, waivers, schedules, welcome packet) become the guide's **Resources** page: each link with Mache's note on when and why to use it.
- Real team data never goes into the code or the repo (same rule as Fleet's seed data). It's entered in the Hub, or imported once from her sheet.
- ⚠️ The Mastersheet has the teams@ mailbox password written in it in plain text. It shouldn't be in a shared sheet; it should move to a password manager. The Hub won't store it.

## Hosts, the guide and handing over

Mache and Michelle are responsible for visiting teams, but other staff host teams too, and this may change soon. So (the Operations rule — see `operations-plan.md`):

- **The lead is a role in the Hub, not a name in the code.** *Visiting teams lead* is held today by Mache and Michelle. Whoever holds it sees every team, approves notices, edits the guide and its resources, and can hand teams between hosts. It's given and taken away on the Hub (Operations → Visiting teams → People) by an admin or the current lead, so when someone steps out and someone else steps in, the role moves and everything follows it. (Every Operations area gets a lead role like this.)
- **Each team has a host**, who can be anyone on the Operations roster. A host sees and works their own team's checklist. The morning message tells each host about their teams.
- **The guide** starts from Mache's document, Canva timeline and Mastersheet: the ten steps for both sides, the stages, the pastoral care framework, wild cards, the resources, and the know-how around them (who the kitchen parents are, which rooms, which drivers). Only the lead edits it; everyone in Operations can read it.
- **Joining as a host:** read the guide, co-host one team with Mache or Michelle, then host one with them looking on.
- **Taking over** (a team, or the whole role): the person leaving writes a note on each team they hold — where it stands, what was promised, who to call — and on the handover date those teams move to the new person.
